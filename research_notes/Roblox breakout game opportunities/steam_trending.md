# Steam / PC indie trends (Oct to Nov 2026) and mechanics that could move to Roblox

Research date: 2026-11-20. Method: WebSearch only. Direct page fetches failed because the session's network blocks thegamer.com, dexerto.com, streetinsider.com and kotaku.com, so most figures below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages and were not read in full. SteamDB live charts were not opened. Treat every number as a dated snapshot that should be checked on SteamDB before it is quoted in a final deliverable.

## 1. What is surging on Steam in Oct to Nov 2026 (CCU, sales, launches, demos)?

### Takeaway
The clear indie breakouts of the last 4 weeks are **Probably Stolen** (cyberpunk black-market shopkeeper sim, about 184k peak CCU on Nov 7, still climbing in week 2), **Worst Class Ever** (1 teacher vs 7 students party game, 600k sold in week one, 30k+ CCU, launched Nov 10) and **ROB RUN** (free-to-play crime extraction MMO from Kefir, launched Nov 4; peak CCU figures are disputed). The summer friendslop hit **Meccha Chameleon** (paint-yourself hide and seek, about 340k all-time peak in June) is the reference point for how fast Roblox copies a Steam hit.

### Cited Findings

**Probably Stolen (Questing Goose Studio / tinyBuild), launched Oct 28, 2026**
- Loop: you run a shop in the "Lower Levels" of a cyberpunk space station, buying and reselling goods (many of them stolen), with choices shaped by faction loyalty and morality. Reviewers compare it to *Papers, Please* and *Cyberpunk 2077*. — [Wikipedia: Probably Stolen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probably_Stolen)
- About 200,000 copies sold on launch day and 112,145 peak CCU in the first days, a tinyBuild publisher record (article dated Oct 30, 2026). — [TechTimes, 2026-10-30](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/329792/20261030/probably-stolen-breaks-tinybuild-record-112145-peak-concurrent-players-steam.htm)
- All-time peak 184,461 CCU on Nov 7, 2026 (SteamDB-derived), still climbing in its second week instead of sliding after day one; over 10,000 Steam reviews, "Overwhelmingly Positive". — [Shane the Gamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/news/probably-stolen-hits-184461-steam-players-and-is-still-climbing-in-its-second-week/)
- GameDiscoverCo newsletter headline says it reached up to 750k copies sold in 5 days (headline only; full text not read). — [GameDiscoverCo newsletter](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/)
- Why it spread: it began as a demo called *Deepspace Emporium* that peaked at 28 players. tinyBuild reworked visuals, marketing and key mechanics, renamed it, and the new demo drew about 660,000 players at Steam Next Fest (tinyBuild figure, via 80.lv). — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/this-indie-cyberpunk-shopkeeper-sim-sells-200-000-copies-in-hours); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/probably-stolen/indie-success-new-name)
- It was the most-played demo of the October 2026 Steam Next Fest, with a fest peak of 39,696 CCU by one hourly tracker. Wikipedia gives 34,230 and 80.lv nearly 40,000, so the sources conflict. — [outputlag.com Next Fest tracker](https://outputlag.com/news/steam-next-fest-october-2026-the-most-played-demos-by-the-numbers/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probably_Stolen)

**Worst Class Ever (Funcore Games; some sources credit Elegoose Games / 2P Games), launched Nov 9 or 10, 2026**
- Loop: a 1 vs 7 asymmetric party game. One player is a strict teacher and up to seven players are mischievous students who cause trouble without getting caught, across 10 stages (classroom, schoolyard and others) in timed rounds that last until the next break. — [Automaton Media, 2026-11-11](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20261111-478713/)
- More than 30,000 concurrent players on Steam. — [Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20261111-478713/)
- Publisher says more than 600,000 copies sold in the first week (company-reported). — [Gamespress press release](https://www.gamespress.com/Worst-Class-Ever-Surpasses-600000-Sales-in-Its-First-Week-Releases-Fir)
- It was the #2 (some lists say #1) most-played demo at October 2026 Next Fest, with a 27,684 fest peak. — [outputlag.com](https://outputlag.com/news/steam-next-fest-october-2026-the-most-played-demos-by-the-numbers/); [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/steam-next-fest-was-flooded-with-slop-these-50-demos-cut-through-the-noise/)

**ROB RUN (Kefir), free-to-play Early Access, launched Nov 4, 2026**
- Loop: a free-to-play "crime MMO" and extraction shooter set in the heist city of Looty Bay, with a desert of player-owned settlements, players acting as police, vehicles, base production and territory. A seven-day wipe cycle (the "Federal Raid") resets plots, bases, weapons and cash; cosmetics and some progress carry over. Worlds are built for about 5,000 players. Monetisation is cosmetic only (B-Coins). — [robrunstatus.com](https://robrunstatus.com/what-is-rob-run); [igamemag review](https://igamemag.com/rob-run-review/); [trtc.io](https://trtc.io/blog/details/rob-run-steam)

### Inferences
- The two biggest late-2026 breakouts follow proven formulas. Probably Stolen is a shop sim with moral and contraband choices (a Supermarket Simulator / Schedule I hybrid plus Papers, Please). Worst Class Ever is a 1 vs many asymmetric social game in the line of hide and seek and Piggy-style games. Both took off from **Next Fest demo momentum**, and Probably Stolen also had a rebrand.
- Probably Stolen's CCU rose through week 2 instead of dropping off. That points to word of mouth and streamers keeping players, not just launch hype.
- ROB RUN's "547k CCU" should not be cited as fact. The only SteamDB-attributed number found is about 23.6k at launch.

## 2. Which indie hits and concepts are viral on Twitch, YouTube, TikTok and X right now?

### Takeaway
Social-video virality in Oct to Nov 2026 is driven by **one-idea concepts that look good in a clip**: Needle In A Haystack Simulator (40M+ views, unreleased), Escape the Walls (wall punching, 4.5M views in 2 days, unreleased) and Abyssal Lapidary (satisfying gem cutting). These get cloned on Steam and Roblox within days, often before the original ships. On Twitch, indie viewership grew 51% YoY, led by Meccha Chameleon, Slay the Spire 2 and Big Walk.

### Cited Findings
- **Needle In A Haystack Simulator** (Studio Bitdot) is a deliberately tedious incremental sim. You sift about 5 million pieces of hay by hand to find one needle, and sifting also turns up items you can sell. It went viral on social media (40M+ views on X) before launch and is listed for **Q4 2026** on Steam. Copycats flooded Steam ("A Needle in a Haystack", "CO-OP Needle Hunt…"), and the creator publicly apologised. PC Gamer called the result "haystack slop". — [fixgamingchannel](https://www.fixgamingchannel.com/needle-in-a-haystack-simulator-viral/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/the-robloxification-of-steam-creator-of-viral-game-apologizes-after-slop-rip-offs-flood-valves-store/); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-great-haystack-slop-is-a-thing-now/); [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/5159870/)
- **Escape the Walls** (Pandorio Games): a prototype video posted on X on Oct 14, 2026 shows a player punching through walls bare-handed and clearing the broken blocks to escape. It had about 4.5M views by Oct 16. A Roblox copy was live **less than 25 hours** after the video, and some Roblox creators said they used AI tools (Claude was named) to build clones. — [GameBusiness.jp, 2026-10-17](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/10/17/28355.html); [Shazoo, 2026-10-19](https://shazoo.ru/2026/10/19/192648); [gamewave.fr](https://gamewave.fr/roblox/roblox-pille-sans-vergogne-un-jeu-viral-en-developpement/)
- **Abyssal Lapidary**: a Victorian gem-cutting sim with abyssal-horror flavour, in which you inherit a failing gem house. It is "making the social media rounds", and PC Gamer called cutting gems "just as satisfying as it looks" but sometimes slow. It is unreleased, with the date TBA. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/abyssal-lapidary-is-making-the-social-media-rounds-and-i-can-confirm-cutting-gemstones-is-just-as-satisfying-as-it-looks/)
- Twitch State of Gaming 2026 (Jan 1 to Sep 1, 2026): indie-tagged channels grew 51% YoY. Meccha Chameleon, Slay the Spire 2 and Big Walk drove more than 348M hours watched, and the report notes growing interest in horror. — [Twitch blog, 2026-09-09](https://blog.twitch.tv/en/2026/09/09/twitch-state-of-gaming-2026/); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/twitchs-state-of-gaming-2026-report-shows-growing-interest-in-indies-horror-games-exclusive/)
- TwitchMetrics Nov 2026 peak-viewer list: Slay the Spire II about 37,184 peak viewers (#144), with Moji Blade and Escape from Playtime nearby. — [TwitchMetrics](https://www.twitchmetrics.net/games/peak?page=3)
- A user comment says streamers and YouTubers helped push Worst Class Ever. This is anecdotal. — [search summary of Steam community / press coverage](https://www.gamespress.com/Worst-Class-Ever-Surpasses-600000-Sales-in-Its-First-Week-Releases-Fir)
- A TikTok creator account (@friendslopgaming) posts monthly "new co-op games" roundups using the #friendslop tag, which shows the label now works as a discovery category. — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@friendslopgaming/video/7691019844119989517)

### Inferences
- The viral unit is now a 10 to 20 second clip of a single satisfying or absurd action (sifting hay, punching walls, cutting gems, painting yourself to hide). Those are exactly the concepts Roblox developers can rebuild in a day, so the lead time for an "original" Roblox take has fallen from weeks to hours.
- Streamer-friendly social deduction or asymmetric formats with chaotic voice moments (Worst Class Ever, Meccha Chameleon) keep players longer than single-gag clip games.

- Big Walk's mechanics and release date were not checked in this session.

## 4. Which mechanics are already copied on Roblox, and which are still open?

### Takeaway
Roblox now copies viral PC concepts within hours to weeks. Meccha Chameleon, Escape the Walls, R.E.P.O., PEAK and Librarian are all already cloned, and some clones out-draw the originals. The **least-adapted current hits** are Probably Stolen's black-market shopkeeper / contraband-and-faction loop and Worst Class Ever's teacher vs students mischief format. These look like the best openings, but whether Roblox clones of them already exist was **not verified**.

### Cited Findings
- **Meccha Chameleon (already saturated):** Roblox clones had more daily peak players than the Steam original after about 3 weeks (July 2026). Kotaku described a "Roblox clone machine" in overdrive, including "Backrooms Meccha Chameleon". — [GamesRadar, 2026-07-16](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/roblox-clones-of-meccha-chameleon-have-more-players-than-the-original-steam-sensation-after-just-3-weeks/); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/more-people-are-playing-meccha-chameleon-knock-offs-than-the-original-hit-steam-game-as-the-roblox-clone-machine-goes-into-overdrive-2000715371)

- **Escape the Walls (already saturated):** Roblox copies appeared within 25 hours. Rotrends tracks Roblox games such as "[🚀] Escape The Walls" and "CH2 Punch Walls To Escape". — [GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/10/17/28355.html); [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10770355219/Escape-The-Walls); [Rotrends](https://www.rotrends.com/game/10770256752/CH2-Punch-Walls-To-Escape)
