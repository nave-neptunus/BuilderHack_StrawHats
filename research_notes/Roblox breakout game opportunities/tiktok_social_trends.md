## 2. What new memes, characters, slang or "brainrot" took off in Oct–Nov 2026, and which are still rising?

### Takeaway
The clearest new kid-friendly character meme of the window is **Prilili (the laughing/gossiping AI fairies)**, which broke out around Oct 5–6, 2026, went mega-viral in Latin America/Brazil, and has already spawned a Roblox game and a microdrama series. **"Remember November 2026" (singing AI polar bear)** is literally having its moment this month. Dictionary.com's 2026 Word of the Year is **"maxxing"** (2025's was "67"). **Verity** (Minecraft horror character) was the big Gen Alpha obsession of Sep–early Oct and is likely past peak.

### Inferences

- **Verity** is past its September peak but the smiley-face AI villain is a recognizable horror archetype for Gen Alpha; a Verity-style AI-assistant-turns-evil horror game could still work but would be late.
