## 1. Which genres are rising, saturated or dying in late 2026 (developer/analyst view)

### Takeaway
"Steal/collect + base-defense" loops (brainrot lineage, now Steal An Egg) and pet/collect games still hold the top of the charts, but the brainrot category's flagship is declining and analysts say pure clones are saturated; the commonly cited openings are hybrids (brainrot theme on fighting/tower-defense/shooter mechanics), Steam-indie-hit adaptations, and social/hangout games. Very little Sept-Nov 2026 analyst material was findable; most genre commentary is from SEO-style blogs and one DevForum ML report.

## 2. Pattern behind the 2025-2026 mega-hits and how fast clones appear

### Takeaway
Mega-hits share a simple, social, collect-and-show-off loop that is easy to clip for TikTok/YouTube, launch small and explode within weeks, and are followed by clones within weeks; in 2026 the same loop got re-skinned (Steal An Egg) and sequelized (Grow a Garden 2), and Steam indie hits got cloned on Roblox within about three weeks, with clones out-drawing the originals. AI tooling is now used by most top games, which compresses build time.

## 3. Platform changes in 2025-2026 affecting discovery and monetization

### Takeaway
The single biggest change for new launches is the 2026 Recommended For You (RFY) rework that rewards 28-day retention (play days, playtime, play-through rate, low first-play bounce) over short-term monetization; developers of short, quick-play games reported 70-80% impression drops from April 2026. Platform-wide, DAU fell for the first time (Q3 2026: 138M, -9% YoY), chat now requires age checks, Roblox Plus replaced Premium, an 18+ US DevEx rate was added, and rewarded video ads moved to a native-only format.

### Cited Findings
**Discovery algorithm**
- Roblox's official RFY update: retention measured across Day 1, Day 2-7 and Day 8-28 (signals expanded from 7 to 28 days); qualified play-through rate removed; play-through rate (PTR) and first-play bounce rate added — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575); [Zehn Studio summary](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/recommended-for-you-retention-update/)
- Discovery FAQ: PTR, first-play bounce rate, play days per user and playtime per user are the most important signals; signals are per-user averages, not totals; "very unlikely" to have a strong Day 8-28 signal if Day 1 and Day 2-7 are poor — [Creator Docs Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq); [Creator Docs Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- Roblox newsroom (June 2026), "Optimizing discovery: how great games reach millions of players": RFY based on objective metrics plus player preferences; will keep refining to recognize long-term value — [Roblox newsroom Jun 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- Q2 2026 shareholder letter (as quoted by press): the change "intentionally provides more impressions for highly retentive games at the expense of near term monetization"; press tied a ~$9B market-cap drop to it — [God is a Geek, Aug 2026](https://godisageek.com/2026/08/roblox-9-billion-algorithm-change/)
- DevForum "How we are improving Home this year": Home now rewards durable games, not just novel ones — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571?page=5)
- Developer reports: Home impressions and CCU down 70-80% from April 11, 2026 after RFY test; complaints that changes "disliked short single experience games"; one dev 1-2K CCU to 200-400 in 4 days; "constant cycle" of impression redistribution (Aug 2026); impressions from 2-3K/hr to 200 despite good PTR and bounce — [DevForum Apr 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/home-recommendation-impressions-and-ccu-dropped-70-80-following-rfy-algorithm-test-april-10-2026/4576807); [DevForum "Huge CCU drop"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/update-huge-ccu-drop-across-all-my-games/4731070); [DevForum impressions tanked](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/impressions-tanked-to-200-after-having-2k-3k-per-hour-game-has-very-good-ptr-as-well-as-very-low-bounce-rates/4841290)
- Third-party claim (unverified against Roblox primary): April 2026 algorithm update filtering "brain rot" games — [creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/roblox-algorithm-update-filtering-brain-rot-games-april-2026); X article "The End of Cash Grab Roblox Games" — [EvanZir on X](https://x.com/EvanZir/article/2070170318396608699)
- Q3 2026 call: 40% of the weekly top 100 RFY games by impressions were created in the last three months (another transcript: 40% of top 1,000 by hours launched in last 90 days); highest seasonally adjusted platform retention since 2021, credited to discovery changes; Q2 letter: top 10 games ~20% of hours vs 30% three years earlier — [Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/roblox-rblx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/); [Benzinga transcript](https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/10/62722787/roblox-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript)

**Platform health (Q3 2026, reported Oct 29, 2026)**

**Monetization / payouts**
- Roblox Plus launched April 30, 2026 (~$4.99/mo), replacing new Premium sign-ups: 10% off Robux purchases (20% from 3rd consecutive month); creators paid for subscriptions started from their experience (up to 250 Robux per sub) and for paid private-server time — [Roblox newsroom Apr 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/introducing-roblox-plus-subscription); [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-plus/4567894); [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/13/roblox-plus-creator-payouts)

### Inferences
- For a new game, Day-1 PTR/bounce and Day 2-7 return rate now gate how many Home impressions it gets; "quick viral gimmick" games that spike and churn are structurally disadvantaged versus 2025. A launch needs a reason to come back daily (timed growth, daily rewards, collection, social) from version 1.
- Yet 40% of top RFY games are under 3 months old, so newcomers are not shut out; the algorithm still surfaces new games that retain.
- Shrinking DAU and payers plus GTA VI (launched Nov 19) mean competition for attention in late Nov is tougher; late December is the bookings peak.
- 18+ DevEx and Roblox's "18+ marketing" push favor R15, older-skewing games, but that is a separate strategy from the kid-driven viral hits.

## 5. Fastest path to a breakout for small/solo devs right now

### Takeaway
The evidence available is thin and mostly vendor/blog-sourced: ship a small, retention-first game fast (often AI-assisted), iterate on Day-1 PTR and bounce, post short clips several times a week on TikTok/YouTube Shorts, and use Roblox Sponsored Experience ads to seed traffic for the algorithm. Re-theming a proven loop or adapting a hot Steam indie title within ~3 weeks is the most evidenced fast route.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok cadence: minimum 3 posts/week, 4-5 optimal; boost only proven posts (Spark Ads); paid reach can be volatile (one studio's sponsored TikTok views fell from ~426K to 6K over months) — [CloutBoost 2026](https://www.cloutboost.com/blog/tiktoks-changing-landscape-for-game-marketing-in-2026-what-developers-need-to-know)
- Post daily or several times a week while testing; match video post dates against Creator Hub analytics; give each creator a unique code; label sponsored posts — [GameSpace](https://gamespace.com/all-articles/news/roblox-tiktok-marketing-working-guide/); vendor claim (unsourced) that 3+ short videos/day yields ~4x visit rate — [BLOXG](https://bloxg.com/roblox-developer-marketing)
- Sponsored Experience ads seeded the 5 newest top-20 earners (Sept 2025, older) — [DevForum RDC recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106)
- Algorithm rewards PTR, low first-play bounce, play days and playtime per user — [Creator Docs Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- Steam-hit adaptations overtook the originals within ~3 weeks — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/roblox-clones-of-meccha-chameleon-have-more-players-than-the-original-steam-sensation-after-just-3-weeks/)
- AI-built games (Build, Studio MCP) reached the top 20 by CCU in Q3 2026 — [ProGameGuides](https://progameguides.com/roblox/ai-made-roblox-games-are-already-breaking-into-the-top-20/)
- Plants vs Brainrots (2025) launched quietly and broke out ~6-8 weeks later after doubling daily players two weeks in a row — [Max Power Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/roblox-s-latest-breakout-game-is-plants-vs-brainrots)

### Inferences
- A practical 20-day play: take a proven loop (collect/steal/defend, pets/eggs, physics party, sports) with a fresh holiday or meme collectible; build MVP in days with AI tools; launch with a daily-return hook; buy a small Sponsored Experience budget to generate enough RFY data; push clips daily; ship updates weekly (popular games' Halloween updates show event updates drive spikes).
- Because impressions now lag 28-day retention data, games launched in late Nov may only get full algorithmic lift by late Dec, which lines up with the late-December bookings peak.
