# Roblox Charts and Fastest-Rising Experiences (snapshot as of 2026-11-20)

**Method note for the report writer.** This session's network blocked direct page fetches. Every request to ggrecon.com, about.roblox.com and livecounts.nl failed with "host not allowed". All findings below therefore come from WebSearch result summaries of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Treat each number as a third-party tracker snapshot. Snapshot times differ between trackers, and the trackers often disagree with each other. Roblox does not publish CCU officially, and roblox.com/charts itself could not be read. Ranks are only cited where a tracker says it mirrors the official chart.

## 1. Current top games by CCU and on the official-style charts

Steal An Egg is the #1 Roblox game by live CCU in mid-November 2026, with roughly 430K to 1.5M concurrent players depending on the tracker and time. Below it, the established games (Brookhaven RP, Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits) sit at about 165K to 435K, and Throw Food For Animals is the newest giant, at 184K to 344K. On the Top Earning chart, RIVALS, Steal An Egg and Blox Fruits lead.

## 2. Recently released games (last ~60 days) with the steepest CCU growth

### Takeaway
Since September, the clear new breakouts are DONT SPLASH, a poolrooms horror obby, and Survive Your Ideas, a word-prompt party survival game launched Oct 6. Both peaked at about 88K to 112K CCU. Behind them is a cluster of ragdoll-physics fighting and crash games, plus "egg" games trying to ride Steal An Egg's success.

### Cited Findings
**GGRecon "10 fastest-growing new Roblox games (November 2026)"** (published about Nov 3; ranks 127 games launched since September, pulled from Roblox's discovery charts on Nov 2):
- #1 DONT SPLASH, "averaging nearly ten million visits a day" in its first 17 days. Bike Universe is also in the top 10 ("the best bike game on Roblox"). Three of the 127 new games have "egg" in the name, and two made the top 10; one is reportedly "Break and Steal an Egg", described as the biggest new game this month by total visits. Twelve of the 127 new games have "ragdoll" in the title (Ragdoll Playground, Ragdoll MMA, Ragdoll Fight, Ragdoll Sword Battles, two Ragdoll Boxing games), and the article calls this a genuine trend. — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/) (full top-10 list not retrievable)

**DONT SPLASH** (horror obby set in "poolrooms", 1–10 players per server, free):
- Visits: 237.2M (Bloxron, Nov 7, 2026). Rolimons shows 339.9M, which the search summary flagged as a likely outlier, and earlier snapshots were lower.
- CCU: about 59K to 112K by snapshot; peak 88,155 on Oct 24 per one site; 27,753 players on Rolimons at capture.
- Creator is disputed: slo7th, Bacon Theory or DoToVerify.
- — [Bloxron](https://bloxron.com/dont-splash); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/119958473240233); [dontsplash.com](https://dontsplash.com/); [newtoplay](https://newtoplay.com/games/dont-splash)

**Survive Your Ideas** (SlayHatt / Survive Your Ideas group):
- Launched Oct 6, 2026. Each round a random player types a word that becomes the in-game hazard or event. Servers hold up to 40 players.
- 123.75M visits. All-time peak 107,836 CCU (Rolimons); window peak 104,723 on Nov 14. Live snapshots of about 30K to 51K CCU.
- Grew +49.5% in average CCU week over week. A fan wiki says it went from outside the top 100 to #3 on third-party global charts in three days in early November.
- Group has about 1.77M members. Renamed "[🗺️MAPS] Survive Your Ideas" on Nov 15, signalling a maps update. Rating about 87–89%.
- — [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/77900175935766); [Rival Ducks](https://rivalducks.com/apps/radar/games/10769671316-survive-your-ideas); [newtoplay](https://newtoplay.com/games/survive-your-ideas); [surviveyourideas.net](https://www.surviveyourideas.net/)

**Ragdoll Physics 🩸**: #2 on BloxRant New & Trending, 29,309 current players, 44.39M+ visits. — [BloxRant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-trending-games); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/121868512253414)

**Realistic Ragdoll MMA**: a physics-based cage fighter with punches, kicks, throws and flying knees, built for PC, controller and mobile. It is in the Up-and-Coming top 3 (Nov 18). — [Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)

**Throw Food For Animals** (Goofy Chupacabra):
- Core loop: throw food (power meter 0–20) to attract animals from zones, then collect them for income.
- Upgrade food tiers (42–50 foods, 19 tiers) and train Throw Power on pull-up stands.
- Pets carry mutations from Gold 1.5x to Rainbow 20x.
- Content: 148 animals, 17 areas. As of Oct 31 it had no code system.
- Launch date not confirmed in sources, but it now sits in the Top 5 CCU (184K on Nov 18).
- — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/throw-food-for-animals-a-beginner-s-guide); [throwfoodforanimals.wiki](https://www.throwfoodforanimals.wiki/); [Eldorado mutations](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/roblox-en/throw-food-for-animals-mutations-sizes/); [Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)

**Roblox-curated new games** (newsroom "New Standout Games to Check Out on Roblox This Month", Nov 16, 2026) include Adventure Time RPG, an IP re-creation of the Cartoon Network series. Full list not retrievable. — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/11/new-standout-games-to-check-out-on-roblox-this-month)

### Inferences
- New breakout formats in Oct–Nov 2026:
  1. Liminal or horror obbies built on a single mechanical gimmick (DONT SPLASH: don't touch the water).
  2. Social party survival driven by player-generated content (Survive Your Ideas: player-typed words become hazards). It is "AI-like" emergent variety that streams well.
  3. Physics/ragdoll combat and crash sandboxes.
  4. "Throw/launch X to collect Y" collection simulators with mutation multipliers, which is the Grow a Garden mutation layer grafted onto a throwing incremental.
- The ragdoll cluster lines up with a Roblox platform change. Roblox began testing native ragdoll death physics in Studio templates (around March 2026), which makes ragdoll games much cheaper to build. Expect even more saturation there.

### Gaps
- Exact release dates for Throw Food For Animals, Ragdoll Physics 🩸, South Hell and Bike Universe were not found.
- No day-by-day CCU growth curves; only peaks and point snapshots.
- The full GGRecon top-10 list and the identity of the second "egg" game could not be retrieved.

## 3. Small (<~20K CCU) but fast-growing games

### Takeaway
The best small-but-rising signals are ragdoll and physics titles (Ragdoll Frenzy, Realistic/Technical Ragdoll MMA, BloxNG crash physics), the horror/odd-themed South Hell (about 10.8K), sports-physics games (Volley Bomb about 18K, Practical Basketball Top 15), and team PvP "T1" titles on Up-and-Coming (Bunker vs Verity T1). Week-over-week growth figures are scarce. Ragdoll Frenzy's "+63% heat" is the only explicit one found below 20K.

### Cited Findings
- Ragdoll Frenzy: "Surging +63%" heat rating, peak 5,108 CCU on Nov 14, 2026. — [Beyond Average Trendwatch](https://explorer.beyondaverage.io/game/10774452423/ragdoll-frenzy)
- South Hell: #3 on BloxRant New & Trending (last 28 days), 10,817 players. — [BloxRant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-trending-games)
- Volley Bomb: #50 on the Nov 18 most-played chart with 18,142 CCU. — [Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)
- BloxNG [CRASH PHYSICS], Realistic Ragdoll MMA and Technical Ragdoll MMA: top of Up-and-Coming on Nov 18 (CCU not given). — [Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)
- Huss Valley T1 (about 54K, so above the "small" cutoff) and Bunker vs Verity T1 on the Up & Coming chart. — [Rival Ducks](https://rivalducks.com/apps/radar/charts/up-and-coming)
- Ragdoll Research (physics lab by "Shenanigans") and Ragdoll Playground (physics sandbox, weapons, chaos) are listed as fresh games on a new-games radar. — [freshgameradar Ragdoll Research](https://freshgameradar.com/games/ragdoll-research/); [freshgameradar Ragdoll Playground](https://freshgameradar.com/games/ragdoll-playground/)
- Roblox native ragdoll death physics: Studio-template change replacing legacy joints with constraints, with live rollout expected later in 2026 (X trending, about March 2026, so older). — [X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2037007015701017020)

### Inferences
- Why they are growing:
  1. Ragdoll physics is cheap to build after the Studio change and makes highly clippable, TikTok-friendly content.
  2. MMA and boxing give it a skill and PvP wrapper.
  3. Crash physics (BeamNG-like) is a proven YouTube format moving onto Roblox.
- The ragdoll cluster is already crowded (12 of 127 new games). The window to win is execution and progression depth (a ranked ladder, cosmetics, a pet or mutation meta), not the theme itself.

### Gaps
- No reliable week-over-week CCU % for most sub-20K games. Rolimons, RoMonitor and Rival Ducks game pages could not be fetched to compute it.
- No data on genres such as fishing, tower defense or incremental in the sub-20K rising band for November 2026.

## 4. Breakout formats of 2025–2026: saturated vs still rising

### Takeaway
The 2025 to mid-2026 run was led by "Grow a" farming plus mutations (Grow a Garden, Grow a Garden 2), "Steal a" base-raid collection (Steal a Brainrot, then Steal An Egg in July 2026), and co-op survival (99 Nights in the Forest). "Steal a X" variants are still producing hits (Steal An Egg is #1). Grow-a-style farming has faded at the top, while ragdoll physics, party survival and liminal horror obbies are the formats rising now.

### Cited Findings
- **All-time CCU records:**
  - Steal a Brainrot is the only Roblox game to pass 25M CCU. Grow a Garden was the first to pass 20M. 99 Nights in the Forest peaked at 14.2M. — [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
  - Steal An Egg's peak conflicts between trackers: RoStats has 14,313,917 on Sep 19, 2026; RBLXSTAT has 12,443,225 on Oct 3, 2026. OtakuKart cites 14.2M peak and 6B visits, but "14.2M" is also the figure attributed to 99 Nights, so possible conflation. — [RoStats records](https://rostats.io/records); [RBLXSTAT records](https://rblxstat.com/lists/record-breaking-roblox-games); [OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/robloxs-steal-an-egg-reaches-6-billion-visits-and-14-2-million-peak-concurrent-players/)
- **Steal An Egg profile:**
  - Launched Jul 25, 2026 per most sources; one fandom page says Aug 20, 2026.
  - Developer is disputed: group owner "MisfitsBSthree", Brockodile, or Badr_Omega.
  - Misfits Gaming tweeted that it hit #1 overnight at 371K CCU (undated).
  - Update 12 (New Alien Biome) shipped Nov 7, 2026.
  - — [stealaneggguide.wiki](https://stealaneggguide.wiki/guides/who-made-steal-an-egg/); [Steal an Egg fandom](https://stealanegg.fandom.com/wiki/Steal_an_Egg); [Misfits on X](https://x.com/MisfitsGG/status/2090785862632038706); [event calendar](https://shivam-das-7.myshopify.com/blogs/roblox/roblox-events-november-1-7-2026-start-times-weekly-calendar)
- **July 2026 (older):**
  - Forge Guides (peak players): #1 Grow a Garden 2 at 465K, #2 Animal Hospital (Anomaly) at 381K, a new entry. It names Grow a Garden 2 and Steal a Brainrot Tycoon (#6) as tycoon picks and calls two tycoons in the top 6 a "genre renaissance". — [Forge Guides July 2026](https://forgeguides.com/en/guides/roblox-top-20-most-played-games-july-2026/)
  - The Fandom monthly-visits timeline for July 2026 had +1 Speed Keyboard Escape #1 at 1.9B visits, then Brookhaven and Steal a Brainrot. — [Roblox Fandom timeline 2026](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_the_top_10_most-visited_games_per_month/2026)
  - RoStats had Animal Hospital (Anomaly) as top game for July 2026. — [RoStats](https://rostats.io/charts)
- **Fading momentum (period ambiguous):** Grow a Garden dropped out of the top 10 "by December", and Steal a Brainrot lost about 30% of monthly visits from its August peak. This likely refers to 2025. — [cubix / search summary](https://www.cubix.co/blog/top-10-most-popular-roblox-games/)
- **Bot inflation:** TD Cowen alleged that bots inflated Grow a Garden's algorithmic visibility; Roblox disputed this. Roblox credited Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot for its 2025 bookings beat. — [shanethegamer stats](https://www.shanethegamer.com/research/roblox-statistics/)
- **Egg wave:** At least 3 of 127 Sept–Nov launches have "egg" in the name ("Break and Steal an Egg" among them). — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Saturated:** "Grow a ..." farming clones, brainrot collection clones, generic "Steal a X" with a reskinned collectible (egg clones are already appearing), and +1-speed escape obbies.
- **Still rising (Oct–Nov 2026):**
  - Ragdoll/physics PvP and crash sandboxes, though crowded.
  - Player-prompt party survival (Survive Your Ideas).
  - Single-gimmick liminal horror obbies (DONT SPLASH).
  - "Throw/launch to collect" incrementals with mutation multipliers (Throw Food For Animals).
  - Animal/creature collection layered on a novel verb.
- **Pattern:** each mega-hit pairs a one-verb core action (grow, steal, throw, survive) with a collectible rarity/mutation economy and a social or steal interaction.

### Gaps
- No authoritative analytics-firm saturation analysis (Bloxbiz, Gamefam, RoMonitor blog) for Q4 2026 was found.
- Data on fishing (Fisch successors), tower defense and Dead Rails-style formats in 2026 was not surfaced.
