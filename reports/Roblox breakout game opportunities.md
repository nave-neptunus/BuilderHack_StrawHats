# Copy the gimmick, then keep the players

As of November 20, 2026, the best bets for a new Roblox game split by how much time you have. For a breakout by about November 30, build something fast that rides a trend already moving: a Roblox take on Steam's new teacher-versus-students party game **Worst Class Ever**, a one-rule horror obby in the style of **DONT SPLASH** with a winter twist, or a ragdoll physics game with a holiday hook. For a breakout by about December 10, you have time for games that suit Roblox's retention-heavy algorithm better: a black-market shop game inspired by Steam's **Probably Stolen** and the GTA VI crime moment, a winter "throw to collect" creature game in the style of **Throw Food For Animals**, a prompt-driven party survival game like **Survive Your Ideas**, and an elimination challenge show launched ahead of MrBeast's *Beast Games* season 3 on December 16. Stay away from farming clones, literal brainrot clones, another "Steal a ___" reskin, shooters, and copies of Steam hits that Roblox has already cloned to death. Two facts apply to every idea. Roblox's discovery system now rewards 28-day retention over short spikes, and analysts expect GTA VI (launched November 19) to pull attention for about two weeks, which covers most of the 10-day window. A realistic "pop off" by November 30 means reaching the Up-and-Coming chart or roughly 20K concurrent players. It has happened that fast this autumn: DONT SPLASH peaked about a week after launch. All the numbers below come from search-result snapshots of third-party trackers that often disagree, so check the live charts before you commit.

## A 10-day breakout is possible, but the algorithm now cares about day 28

It helps to set the bar first. On the November 18 most-played snapshot, the **#50 game had about 18,142 concurrent players** ([Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)), so a new game needs roughly 20K to show up on most "most played" lists. The Up-and-Coming and growth charts are easier targets and are where new games get noticed. Fast breakouts do happen. DONT SPLASH went live on October 17 ([dontsplash.com](https://dontsplash.com/)) and one tracker logged a **peak of 88,155 on October 24** ([Bloxron](https://bloxron.com/dont-splash)). Survive Your Ideas took longer: it launched October 6 and climbed to a **104,723 peak on November 14** after a jump of 49.5% in average players in one week ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/77900175935766); [Rival Ducks](https://rivalducks.com/apps/radar/games/10769671316-survive-your-ideas)). The giants took longer still. Steal An Egg was created July 25 and peaked around September 19, about eight weeks later ([RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/game/107778070777162)).

Build speed is no longer the main bottleneck. A Roblox copy of the viral "Escape the Walls" prototype was live **less than 25 hours** after the original video, and some of the people cloning it said they used AI tools, Claude among them ([GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/10/17/28355.html); [Shazoo](https://shazoo.ru/2026/10/19/192648)). Roblox says **more than 80% of its top 100 games** used AI tools in the prior month, and two games built almost entirely with AI reached the top 20 by concurrent players in Q3 ([TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/354373/over-80-of-robloxs-top-100-games-are-ai-assissted-topping-70-000-ai-games)). That cuts both ways: you can ship in days, and so can everyone copying the same trend.

Discovery is the harder part. Roblox's 2026 Recommended For You rework measures retention across day 1, days 2 to 7 and days 8 to 28, and treats play-through rate, first-play bounce, play days and playtime per user as the key signals ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575); [Creator Docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)). Developers of short, quick-play games reported **70 to 80% drops in Home impressions** after the April test ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/home-recommendation-impressions-and-ccu-dropped-70-80-following-rfy-algorithm-test-april-10-2026/4576807)). New games are not shut out: **40% of the weekly top 100 games by recommendation impressions were under three months old** ([Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/roblox-rblx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)). In practice, every idea below needs a reason to come back on day 2 from version 1, such as daily rewards, a collection or a rotating stage.

Timing works against a November launch in one way and for it in another. Roblox's daily users fell 9% year over year in Q3 to 138M, and Bank of America expects GTA VI's hit to Roblox to land in **the first two weeks after the November 19 launch** ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q3-2026-slides-first-user-decline-marks-inflection-point-93CH-4985385); [Yahoo/BofA](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/roblox-rises-4-bofa-target-170607964.html)). On the other side, US Thanksgiving falls on November 26 ([CBS Sports](https://new.cbssports.com/nfl/news/nfl-thanksgiving-schedule-2026-games/)), and Roblox says a large share of Q4 bookings comes in **the last week of December** ([TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/roblox-sinks-14-on-soft-q4-bookings-guide-as-cfo-flags-gta-modest-headwind)). A game launched in late November that holds its players will have its 28 days of retention data right as winter break starts. No official December Roblox platform event has been announced. Fan sources point to dates between December 8 and December 17, all unconfirmed ([TechYorker](https://techyorker.com/?p=849065); [WhyIron on X](https://x.com/WhyIron_/status/2117388221726552152)).

The launch playbook in the notes is thin but consistent: seed traffic with Roblox Sponsored Experience ads (the five newest games to reach the top 20 by earnings in 2025 all started that way, per [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106)), post short clips at least three to five times a week ([CloutBoost](https://www.cloutboost.com/blog/tiktoks-changing-landscape-for-game-marketing-in-2026-what-developers-need-to-know)), and ship updates weekly. Steal An Egg kept up a steady stream of numbered updates and admin-abuse events, with Update 12 landing November 7 ([event calendar](https://shivam-das-7.myshopify.com/blogs/roblox/roblox-events-november-1-7-2026-start-times-weekly-calendar)).

## Ten-day list: four bets that could break out by November 30

These are ordered by how strongly the evidence backs them. All four are fast to build, which is the point: by the time a two-week project ships, the 10-day window is over.

### 1. Teacher vs students mischief game (adapting Worst Class Ever)

**Evidence.** Worst Class Ever launched on Steam on November 9 or 10. Its publisher says it sold **more than 600,000 copies in the first week** ([Gamespress](https://www.gamespress.com/Worst-Class-Ever-Surpasses-600000-Sales-in-Its-First-Week-Releases-Fir)), and it passed **30,000 concurrent players** ([Automaton Media](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20261111-478713/)). It was one of the two most-played demos at October's Steam Next Fest ([outputlag](https://outputlag.com/news/steam-next-fest-october-2026-the-most-played-demos-by-the-numbers/)). One player is a strict teacher, up to seven are students causing trouble without getting caught, and rounds run until the bell. The timing precedent is Meccha Chameleon, whose Roblox copies passed the Steam original's player count about three weeks after it launched ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/roblox-clones-of-meccha-chameleon-have-more-players-than-the-original-steam-sensation-after-just-3-weeks/)). Worst Class Ever turns three weeks old on November 30.

**Why it could work.** Every Roblox kid knows school, and one-versus-many is already a native Roblox format thanks to games like Piggy. Short rounds full of near misses make good clips and good reasons to queue again. Of everything on Steam right now, this is the hit with the closest fit to Roblox's audience.

**What to build.** One classroom plus a hallway. The teacher has a view cone and a "caught" meter. Students get eight to ten pranks to pull off: draw on the board, swap the teacher's coffee, throw paper balls, hide under desks. Students score for pranks done unseen and the teacher scores for catches. Coins buy cosmetics like backpacks and teacher outfits, and a daily "detention challenge" gives players a reason to come back on day 2. Starting from an asymmetric template, this is about 4 to 6 days of work.

**Risks.** Nobody has checked whether Roblox versions already exist, so search Roblox and Rotrends before you start. If several are live, keep the structure and change the setting (hospital, library, sleepover). Don't copy the original's name or art. PEAK's developers got a Roblox clone called "Cliff" taken down ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-indie-bestseller-PEAK-s-developer-recommends-pirating-the-game-instead-of-playing-a-Roblox-ripoff.1080200.0.html)). Chat now needs an age check ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/01/roblox-age-checks-required-to-chat)), so build the fun around movement and emotes rather than typing.

### 2. One-rule horror obby with a winter setting (the DONT SPLASH pattern)

**Evidence.** In DONT SPLASH, a blind monster hunts players by the sound of splashing water across nine "Poolrooms" stages ([dontsplash.com](https://dontsplash.com/)). It topped GGRecon's November ranking of 127 new games, **averaging nearly ten million visits a day** in its first 17 days ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)). Single TikTok clips reached about 230,900 and 148,100 likes ([TikTok @wildbilly29](https://www.tiktok.com/@wildbilly29/video/7696324998922767647); [TikTok @shaiwans](https://www.tiktok.com/@shaiwans/video/7698508449516211487)). Copycats have already started mixing it with memes, as in "Don't splash Prilili" ([Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/119887375483641/NO-SALPIQUES-a-Prilili)). South Hell, another horror-flavored game, sits third on Roblox's growth chart ([BloxRant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-trending-games)), and Twitch's 2026 report notes growing interest in horror ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/twitchs-state-of-gaming-2026-report-shows-growing-interest-in-indies-horror-games-exclusive/)).

**Why it could work.** "Make a sound and it comes running" explains itself in two seconds of video. Small servers keep it tense, and streamers get real screams out of it. It is the fastest build on this list.

**What to build.** Pick one sense and one winter setting. For example, a snowed-in mall or a frozen lake where the creature tracks the crunch of snow underfoot. Carpet and ice are quiet, running is fast but loud, and players have to choose. Five short stages with checkpoints, one chase sequence and a catch animation made for recording. For day-2 retention, add a new stage every night, a time-trial leaderboard and a badge per stage. That is about 2 to 4 days of work.

**Risks.** Short obbies are exactly what the 2026 algorithm punishes, so the nightly stage and leaderboard are not optional. DONT SPLASH copies are multiplying, and the original can ship its own winter update at any time. Creators tag the clips "only for 16+", which points to an older and smaller slice of Roblox's audience ([TikTok @shaiwans](https://www.tiktok.com/@shaiwans/video/7698508449516211487)).

### 3. Ragdoll sled crash or ragdoll sports game

**Evidence.** On November 18 the top three Up-and-Coming games were all physics games: BloxNG [CRASH PHYSICS], Realistic Ragdoll MMA and Technical Ragdoll MMA ([Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)). Ragdoll Physics was second on Roblox's growth chart with **29,309 players** ([BloxRant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-trending-games)), and Ragdoll Frenzy shows a "+63%" surge ([Beyond Average](https://explorer.beyondaverage.io/game/10774452423/ragdoll-frenzy)). Roblox's move to native ragdoll physics in Studio templates made these much cheaper to build ([X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2037007015701017020)), and analysts count ragdoll clips among the best performers on TikTok ([RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/roblox-youtube-tiktok-content-trends-2026/)).

**Why it could work.** It is the cheapest format to build and the easiest to clip. The catch is that **12 of the 127 games launched since September have "ragdoll" in the title** ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)), so you need a hook beyond the physics.

**What to build.** Two good options. The first is a sled crash: launch down an icy mountain, score by distance and damage, spend points on faster sleds and bigger ramps, with a new course seed every day. The second is a physics sport, which is a less crowded lane. Volley Bomb was #50 with 18,142 players and Practical Basketball was #15 ([Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)), Turbo Soccer and Backseat Rally made GGRecon's top 10 new games ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)), and RoLearn rates sports and racing as less saturated than simulators and obbies ([RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/unsaturated-roblox-game-genres-2026/)). Either takes about 1 to 3 days.

**Risks.** This is the most crowded of the rising formats. The winner will be whoever adds progression (a ranked ladder, cosmetics, unlockable sleds), not whoever picks the best theme. Gory "🩸" framing narrows your audience.

### 4. Meme add-on: Prilili fairies and the November polar bear

**Evidence.** The Prilili "laughing fairies", AI-made clips of two tiny fairies whispering gibberish and giggling, broke out around October 5 and are still spawning microdramas, AI templates and at least one Roblox game ([Trendswap](https://trendswap.app/blog/prilili-meme-explained); [TikTok @fae.eyes](https://www.tiktok.com/@fae.eyes/video/7697382284529241358)). The "Remember November 2026" singing polar bear is peaking this month ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/remember-november-2026-is-coming)). Dictionary.com named **"maxxing"** its 2026 word of the year ([Dictionary.com](https://www.dictionary.com/e/word-of-the-year-2026/)). The year's viral characters keep being AI-generated with nonsense names, which is the same pattern that produced Italian brainrot and Steal a Brainrot.

**Why it could work.** It costs a day or two and can give the clips for ideas 1 to 3 a reason to spread. The notes found no single October or November meme on the scale of "67" or Tung Tung Tung Sahur, so this is a booster, not a foundation.

**What to build.** Don't make a game around it. Add your own fairy designs as rare collectibles or a hidden NPC inside one of the games above, add a singing polar bear as a November-only item, and use "maxxing" in names and badges ("Sledmaxxing").

**Risks.** The November meme expires by design on December 1. Prilili is six weeks old and may already be past its peak. The characters belong to a creator, so draw your own. One unverified source claims Roblox's April update started filtering brainrot games ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/roblox-algorithm-update-filtering-brain-rot-games-april-2026)).

A note on Thanksgiving and Black Friday: the US break (November 26 to 29) and Black Friday (November 27) fall inside this window, but the notes found no data on how much Roblox play rises that week. Treat a turkey chase or "Black Friday rush" as a cheap limited event inside one of the ideas above, not as a game of its own.

## Twenty-day list: five bets that could break out by December 10

These need one to two weeks of building, but they fit the retention algorithm better than the 10-day bets. They also launch into the run-up to winter break.

### 1. Black-market shop game with player inspectors (adapting Probably Stolen)

**Evidence.** Probably Stolen, a cyberpunk shop sim where you buy and resell mostly stolen goods while juggling faction loyalty, launched October 28 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probably_Stolen)). It sold about **200,000 copies on launch day** ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/329792/20261030/probably-stolen-breaks-tinybuild-record-112145-peak-concurrent-players-steam.htm)) and hit **184,461 concurrent players on November 7, still climbing in its second week** ([Shane the Gamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/news/probably-stolen-hits-184461-steam-players-and-is-still-climbing-in-its-second-week/)). That kind of week-two climb points to word of mouth, not launch hype. The crime theme is everywhere right now. GTA VI launched November 19 and leads The Game Awards with eight nominations ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-announced)). Roblox's own "The Takeback" event (November 16 to 23) is a heist event run through Emergency Hamburg, Notoriety and Jailbreak ([Roblox Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Takeback)). Kefir's free crime MMO ROB RUN launched November 4 with a SteamDB-recorded peak of 23,641 ([StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/GetNews/ROB+RUN+Reaches+547,937+Concurrent+Steam+Players+as+Fan+Database+robrungame.com+Tracks+Its+Rise/27357828.html)).

**Why it could work.** Most of Roblox's audience can't play GTA VI but sees it all over YouTube and TikTok. Buying, pricing and reselling fits Roblox's trading culture, and a shop that restocks daily gives a natural day-2 reason. Shop sims exist on Roblox, but the notes found no contraband-and-inspection twist.

**What to build.** Each player runs a stall in a neon market. Buy mystery crates from a fence NPC, inspect each item (real or fake, flagged or clean), price it for NPC and player buyers, and upgrade the stall. Every few minutes an inspection round turns some players into inspectors who search stalls and confiscate flagged goods for a reward, which adds bluffing and player interaction. Reputation with two rival factions unlocks better crates. Keep the tone cartoonish, more "smuggled space junk" than real crime. About 7 to 12 days.

**Risks.** RoLearn lists tycoons among the most saturated genres ([RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/unsaturated-roblox-game-genres-2026/)), so the inspection and bluffing have to be the hook rather than the shop. Cops-and-robbers games are an established Roblox genre with big incumbents like Jailbreak. Nobody checked whether a Roblox Probably Stolen copy already exists.

### 2. Winter throw-to-collect creature game (the Throw Food For Animals pattern)

**Evidence.** Throw Food For Animals was **#4 on Roblox with 184,028 concurrent players** on November 18 ([Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts)). The loop: throw food with a power meter to lure animals from zones, collect them for income, upgrade food tiers and train throw power ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/throw-food-for-animals-a-beginner-s-guide)), with mutations from Gold at 1.5x to Rainbow at 20x ([Eldorado](https://www.eldorado.gg/blog/roblox-en/throw-food-for-animals-mutations-sizes/)). Last December the holiday winners were existing games that added Christmas events, such as 99 Nights in the Forest's candy-cane currency and Fisch's "Fischmas" ([games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-top-games-and-trends-december-2025/); [The BloxBit](https://thebloxbit.blogspot.com/2025/12/roblox-christmas-event-2025-festive.html)). Cats keep showing up too: Cat Battles made GGRecon's top 10 new games ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/fastest-growing-new-roblox-games-november-2026/)) and the cat sudoku game Meowdoku climbed the mobile charts ([FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/global-mobile-game-rankings-october-2026-top-15-downloads-revenue/)).

**Why it could work.** Every recent mega-hit pairs one simple verb (grow, steal, throw) with a rarity and mutation economy. Collection plus daily rewards is the best match for the 28-day retention signal of anything on this list, and a winter theme gives a natural daily calendar.

**What to build.** Throw snowballs or wrapped presents with a power meter to lure winter creatures like penguins, cats in scarves, reindeer and a rare yeti. Collected creatures earn passive income, players train throw power, and mutations (Frosted, Gilded, Aurora) multiply value. An advent calendar running December 1 to 25 gives a daily gift, and trading gives players something to show off. About 7 to 10 days, with AI tools covering much of the creature modeling.

**Risks.** Throw Food For Animals is far ahead and will probably ship its own winter update. Copy the loop too closely and you look like a reskin, so change one big thing, either the verb or the setting. Simulators are the most crowded genre on Roblox ([RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/unsaturated-roblox-game-genres-2026/)).

### 3. Prompt-card party survival (the Survive Your Ideas pattern)

**Evidence.** In Survive Your Ideas, which launched October 6, a random player types a word each round and it becomes the hazard everyone has to survive, with up to 40 players per server. It hit an **all-time peak of 107,836**, grew **49.5% in average players week over week**, and was renamed on November 15 for a maps update ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/77900175935766); [Rival Ducks](https://rivalducks.com/apps/radar/games/10769671316-survive-your-ideas)). It sits **#1 on Roblox's growth chart** of the last 28 days ([BloxRant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-trending-games)).

**Why it could work.** Player-made chaos gives endless variety without endless content, and it streams well. Rounds are short and every round is different, which helps both clips and replays.

**What to build.** Skip free typing. Each round, a random player picks one of three hazard cards from a library of 50 or more (lava floor, turkey stampede, snow avalanche, gravity flip, giant stomping snowman). Survivors earn coins for cosmetics, and a pass lets players add their own card to the pool. Holiday cards rotate in through December. About 7 to 12 days, since each hazard is its own small piece of content.

**Risks.** The original is still rising and updating, and TikTok "underrated game" posts already mention a similar-sounding "idea war" ([TikTok #newrobloxgame](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/newrobloxgame?lang=en)), so clones probably exist. Free-typed prompts would need filtering under the age-check chat rules, which is why the card version is safer.

### 4. Elimination challenge show (timed for Hunger Games and Beast Games)

**Evidence.** *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opens November 20 ([Box Office Pro](https://boxofficepro.com/long-range-forecast-the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping/)). MrBeast's *Beast Games* season 3 premieres December 16 on Prime Video with contestants from 196 nations, a $5M prize and new episodes every Wednesday through January 13 ([About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/md/news/entertainment/mr-beast-prime-video-beast-games-season-three)). Season 1 reached 50M viewers in 25 days ([Otakukart](https://otakukart.com/beast-games-world-champions-sets-december-16-premiere-on-prime-video-with-5-million-prize/)). *Avengers: Doomsday* follows on December 18 ([The Numbers](https://www.the-numbers.com/movies/release-schedule?from_mobile=Yes)).

**Why it could work.** The show's weekly schedule gives a month of renewed interest, and eliminations are dramatic in a clip. This idea rests on the calendar rather than on Roblox chart data, so it is the least proven on the list.

**What to build.** A lobby of 24 to 40 players, eight to ten short challenges drawn at random, eliminated players spectating and voting on the next challenge, and the last player standing winning a crown and currency. Hold a weekly "championship" on Wednesday nights to line up with episode drops. Use no MrBeast, Beast Games or Hunger Games names or logos. About 8 to 14 days.

**Risks.** Demand peaks after December 10, so the real plan is to launch by December 10 and collect retention data before the December 16 premiere. Elimination minigame games have been on Roblox for years (this comes from general knowledge, not the notes), so polish and pacing decide it.

### 5. Wildcard: haystack sifting simulator

**Evidence.** Needle In A Haystack Simulator, a deliberately tedious game about sifting about 5 million pieces of hay for one needle, drew **40M+ views on X** before launch and is listed for Q4 2026 ([fixgamingchannel](https://www.fixgamingchannel.com/needle-in-a-haystack-simulator-viral/)). Copycats flooded Steam fast enough that PC Gamer coined "haystack slop" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-great-haystack-slop-is-a-thing-now/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/the-robloxification-of-steam-creator-of-viral-game-apologizes-after-slop-rip-offs-flood-valves-store/)).

**Why it could work.** Dig, sift, sell, upgrade and rebirth is a natural Roblox simulator loop, and it takes only 3 to 5 days to build. Keep it ready to launch the week the Steam game ships.

**Risks.** No release date was found, so the spike might not land in the window at all. The first-mover advantage is already gone on Steam and probably on Roblox.

## How far to trust these numbers

All five research notes say the same thing: the session's network blocked direct page reads for most sources, including GGRecon, the Roblox newsroom, Kotaku and Box Office Mojo. Every figure here comes from search-result summaries of those pages. The trackers disagree widely. Steal An Egg's live player count ranged from **433,730 to about 1.5M** across sites in the same month ([Endgametools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/charts); [livecounts.nl](https://livecounts.nl/roblox-charts/)), and Roblox does not publish official player counts. Some claims were too weak to use: ROB RUN's 547,937 player figure comes from a fan site's press release and should not be trusted. GGRecon's full top-10 list could not be read, and nobody confirmed GTA VI's launch after the fact.

The biggest gaps bear directly on the build decision. No one checked Roblox for existing copies of Worst Class Ever or Probably Stolen, so a ten-minute search there should come before any code. No data turned up on how much Roblox play rises during Thanksgiving week, and the December platform event dates are rumors. The build times are estimates, not measurements. The sources agree on direction more than on numbers: physics, one-rule horror, party survival and Steam adaptations are rising, while farming, brainrot and steal-a reskins are fading. Treat the specific figures as rough guides.

## Conclusion

The pattern that runs through all this data is that the gap between "something goes viral" and "a Roblox version exists" has shrunk to hours, so being first to copy no longer wins on its own. Since the April algorithm change, what decides the winner among a dozen copies of the same idea is whether players come back on day 2 and day 8. That flips the usual advice for this window. A 10-day game should still be built in under a week, but its daily stage, leaderboard or calendar matters more than its polish, because that is what Roblox measures.

A sensible plan for one developer is two tracks. This week, ship one fast bet, the teacher-versus-students game if a Roblox search shows the lane is open or the winter horror obby if it doesn't, and treat it as a cheap test of your clip and ad pipeline over Thanksgiving. In parallel, build the winter throw-to-collect game or the black-market shop for launch by early December. A game launched then reaches its first 28 days of retention data just as winter break and the last-week-of-December spending peak arrive, which is the strongest timing advantage anywhere in this window.
